転倒予防日誌 / Fall Journal

毎日の体調と無理のない体操を、端末内で記録するアプリです。アプリ本体とすべての機能を無料で利用できます。24種の体操、日替わりの座位3種、体調・疲れ具合・メモ、カレンダーとチェックの記録を収録しています。

お問い合わせは misterdevelop.support@gmail.com へ、アプリ名「転倒予防日誌」、端末名、OSとアプリのバージョン、問題が起きる手順をお知らせください。メールアプリが使えない場合は、アドレスをコピーし、Webメールや別の端末をご利用ください。氏名や健康記録の送付は必要ありません。

「今日」→「体操を始める」から安全事項を確認し、「3つ続ける」または「1つずつ」を選びます。途中でも一時停止・終了できます。音声・音楽は個別にオフにでき、教材・記録はオフラインで使えます。体調の悪い日は休み、痛み・めまい・息苦しさなどがあれば中止してください。

Fall Journal support

Record your daily wellbeing and gentle exercise on your device. The app and all features are free. It includes 24 exercises, three seated exercises each day, wellbeing and fatigue entries, notes, a calendar and personal check records.

Email misterdevelop.support@gmail.com with the app name, device model, OS and app versions, and the steps that led to the issue. If email is unavailable, copy the address and use webmail or another device. You do not need to send your name or health records.

Open Today → Start exercising, read the safety checks, then choose Start all three or Start one at a time. You can pause or finish at any time. Voice guidance and music can be switched off separately. Exercise materials and records work offline. Rest when unwell, and stop if you feel pain, dizziness or shortness of breath.

Version 2.0.1 and later support Japanese and English. Choose the app language in your device's Settings. Guidance uses an installed Apple voice for that language. If a suitable voice is unavailable, follow the on-screen instructions. Changing the display language does not translate your own notes or change stored records.

SimpleAcceleration support

Measure and record straight-line walking time, speed, estimated steps and acceleration, and compare records stored on your device. The app and all features are free.

Email misterdevelop.support@gmail.com with the app name, device model, OS and app versions, and the steps that led to the issue. If email is unavailable on this device, copy the address and use webmail or another device. You do not need to send participants' names, photos, videos or measurement records.

Open the Guide tab in the app for instructions. For two-device mode, update both devices to the latest available version and check that their versions match in Settings. Results are reference values, not intended for diagnosis or treatment decisions.
